Aircraft Design

Led conceptual and preliminary design of a fixed-wing aircraft within a small team, covering wing planform definition, airfoil selection, and aerodynamic configuration. Spacecraft Dynamics & Design

Designed and simulated multi-flyby interplanetary trajectories for ten of Jupiter's moons using Python and the REBOUND N-body framework, then applied astrodynamics methods within a full systems-engineering mission concept to Saturn's moon Titan. Led trajectory design while collaborating on spacecraft architecture, deployable satellites, and a submersible concept for Titan's methane lakes.

AstrodynamicsOrbital MechanicsMission DesignPythonSystems Engineering

Navigation Systems

Applied state-space modeling and Kalman filtering to GNSS positioning problems, implementing differential GPS and augmentation methods through a cumulative course project. Gained hands-on experience with estimation structure, error sources, and solution robustness across noisy, uncertainty-driven measurement scenarios.

GNSS / GPSKalman FilteringMATLABOptimal Estimation

MPC for Rocket Booster Landing

Designed and implemented a constrained nonlinear Model Predictive Controller for powered landing of a Falcon 9-class booster, benchmarked against a PD baseline in nominal and wind-disturbed closed-loop simulations. The MPC achieved a 1.453 m landing error while using 18% less fuel than the PD controller, which failed to land in either scenario.
